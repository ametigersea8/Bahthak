# BAHTHAK – Research Consulting Organization

**Research . Publish . Grow**

BAHTHAK (بحثك) is a research consulting organization based in Amman, Jordan. It supports researchers, graduate students and faculty through every stage of academic work, from shaping a research question to preparing a manuscript for submission to an indexed journal.

Founder and Director: **Dr. Hazim Monqeth Haddad**, University Lecturer

---

## Services

| Service | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Research methodology | Designing the study, choosing methods, sampling and instruments |
| Literature reviews | Systematic searching, synthesis, and identifying research gaps |
| Data analysis | Quantitative and qualitative analysis, interpretation of results |
| Academic writing | Structuring, editing and strengthening manuscripts and theses |
| Publication preparation | Journal selection, formatting, and submission readiness for Scopus-indexed journals |
| Training workshops | Workshops on research skills, analysis tools and academic publishing |
| Student guidance | Mentoring for master's and doctoral students |

## Subject areas

- Management and strategic management
- Marketing
- Human resources
- Supply chains and logistics
- Entrepreneurship
- Management information systems (MIS)
- AI and data analytics
- Finance and accounting
